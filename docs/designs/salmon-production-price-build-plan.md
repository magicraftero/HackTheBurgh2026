## Problem Statement

Norway farms just over half the world's Atlantic salmon, and it publishes almost everything that happens in its sea cages. Every month, for each production area, it reports standing biomass, fish numbers, smolt stocking, feed use and deaths. Every week, for every farm, it reports lice counts, delousing treatments, disease and sea temperature. Nobody has openly tested how well that public data forecasts Norway's salmon production (harvest by area, where volume = fish count × average weight) and the export price, or which drivers matter most. The team (four people, about 17 hours at HackTheBurgh XIII, theme "make something previously invisible visible, and predict something") will build those forecasts, prove them on a backtest, rank the drivers and ship a hosted website that shows the findings.

## What Makes This Cool

- **You can see Norway's harvest weeks before Norway publishes it.** Weekly farm reports and export figures for the month just ended are public within about ten days, while the official harvest figures come out around the 20th of the next month.
- **What happens under the water is public but unread.** Biomass, feed, deaths, lice, disease and temperature all sit in open datasets that almost nobody connects to supply and price.
- **Count × weight shows why supply moved.** Fast growth gives more and bigger fish. Early harvests, forced by lice, disease or deaths, give more but smaller fish. Splitting harvest volume into count × weight separates the two.
- **Proof on data it never saw.** The backtest replays real time from 2021. The last 21 months are a locked holdout that nobody scores until the 04:00 freeze. The paid salmon forecasts the search turned up (Vesper, Expana) don't publish an open backtest.
- **A driver ranking.** The site shows which factors actually improve the forecast, measured by how much worse it gets without each one.
- **EUREKA from this session, for the lice driver.** The public lice data we found (Ireland's dashboard, Scotland's and British Columbia's datasets) is all counts per farm, built for checking a legal limit. Farmers treat to stay under that limit, so counts hide the pressure. The lice driver uses mobile lice and treatments instead.

## Recommended Approach

The three back-end people produce every finding as static files in the repo: data as JSON, and each chart as an Altair (Vega-Lite) spec sharing one theme. The front-end person builds a hosted site that shows them, designed by the team later. There is no live server, so nothing can crash during judging, and the BarentsWatch token stays on one laptop.

Each person's coding agent gets two files: `docs/plans/00-shared.md` (the shared contract: paths, schemas, driver definitions, the holdout rule and deadlines) and its own role plan (`data.md`, `production.md`, `price.md` or `frontend.md`).

### How the backtest proves it works

- **It replays real time.** Every month from 2021, the nowcast is made as of the 10th of the following month, using only data published by then. Models are refitted on that data alone.
- **The holdout is locked.**
  - Production: develop on Jan 2021 – Dec 2024, and hold out Jan 2025 – Sep 2026.
  - Price: develop on 2021 – 2024, and hold out 2025-W01 – 2026-W44.
  - Backtest code scores only the development window until the 04:00 freeze. Then it runs once with `--final`, and F1 and F2 are judged on the holdout.
- **It includes a worked example.** September 2026, the newest month the Directorate has published, is shown as the model would have called it on 10 October 2026, set against the official figure, nationally and by area.
- **It's honest about what it measures.** The actual figures are today's revised data. The paid forecasters' accuracy claims couldn't be checked.

### Changes to spec v2

- **The main focus is production and price, with lice as one driver among several** (changed during the session). The added drivers are feed per kg of biomass, deaths, the smolt pipeline, disease (ISA and PD) and USD/NOK. Most come free with files already being loaded.
- **The live test is replaced by a locked holdout and the September 2026 example** (changed during the session). Judging is on Sunday, so predictions checked on 20 Nov or 2 Dec can't count.
- **New test structure:**
  - F1 (primary): does the Drivers model beat baseline B1 (season, biomass and export volume) on the production holdout?
  - F2 (secondary): the price test, v2's H3, now on its holdout.
  - M1 and M2: v2's H1 (lice signature) and H2 (growth), now mechanism tests.
  - M3: v2's H4.
- **Baseline B1 adds the month's export volume** from Statistics Norway's weekly figures. Most national volume is known before the Directorate's release, and a judge from a trading firm will ask.
- **M1 runs on mobile lice, with treatments as the delousing control.** Zhang et al. find harvests speed up as lice rise but slow during delousing, so treatments alone would leave the sign on fish count ambiguous.
- **Pass marks tightened.** Lag windows, controls, driver definitions, nowcast timing and the evaluation windows are all fixed now. The mechanism tests use sums of lag coefficients. Both forecast tests state their MAE threshold and their Clark–West squared-error test.
- **Simpler models.** Direct OLS for each horizon replaces ARIMA errors, and empirical error quantiles replace the Bayesian fan. The answer is the same and it saves hours.
- **National weekly indices are capacity-weighted across all farms**, not biomass-weighted by area. The Directorate's area data starts in October 2017, but price training starts in 2012.

### Data notes

- **Directorate CSV:** `;`-separated UTF-8 with a byte-order mark (`encoding="utf-8-sig"`). Keep `ARTSID == "LAKS"` (the file includes rainbow trout) and sum over stocking year (`UTSETTSÅR`). Biomass, fish numbers, smolt releases, feed and deaths are all in this one file.
- **No areas are hidden.** All 13 appear in all 108 months (Oct 2017–Sep 2026). Suppressed rows sit in a `PO_KODE == "(null)"` row holding 1–5% of harvest. Count it nationally and leave it out of the area analysis.
- **`UTTAK_KG` is round-weight equivalent** (gutted × 1.125 plus round weight), so `UTTAK_KG / UTTAK_STK` is live weight per fish.
- **Statistics Norway 03024** runs to week 44 of 2026. Fresh salmon is `VareGrupper2 = 01`, with `Vekt` (tonnes) and `Kilopris` (NOK/kg).
- **`available_at`:**
  - lice for week w: end of week w + 1;
  - Statistics Norway week w: the next Wednesday;
  - Directorate month m: the 20th of month m + 1;
  - exchange rates: the next day.

  Each ISO week belongs to the month containing its Thursday.
- **A hook to check:** September 2024 had the most fish harvested (42.5 m) and the lowest average weight (4.28 kg) of any September from 2018 to 2026. Confirm it with anomalies before using it.

```text
Pass marks: committed before any results were looked at.

COMMON DEFINITIONS
Units: production area × month, Oct 2017 – Sep 2026, 13 areas (the "(null)" row excluded).
Anomalies: logs, minus a trailing 12-month level (52 weeks for weekly series), Fourier
seasonality and holiday terms (Easter; Christmas for weekly series).
Drivers: biomass and fish numbers; smolt released 12–23 months earlier; feed per kg of
biomass; deaths per 1,000 fish; capacity-weighted mobile lice per fish; treatments per active
farm-week; share of reporting sites with ISA or PD (only if the data exists); sea-temperature
degree-day anomaly = 6-week rolling sum of 7 × (weekly sea temperature − that area's mean for
the same ISO week in previous years), split into bands by the current week's temperature
(below 8 °C, 8–14 °C, 14 °C and above); national export volume.
Nowcast timing: made on the 10th of the following month, using only data published by then.
Baselines: B0 = season plus biomass and fish numbers at the end of the previous month.
B1 = B0 plus that month's national export volume. Drivers = B1 plus every other driver.

EVALUATION WINDOWS AND THE HOLDOUT
Every forecast test is a walk-forward backtest that simulates real time: each forecast uses
only data published by its forecast date, and models are refitted on that data alone.
Production: development window Jan 2021 – Dec 2024; holdout Jan 2025 – Sep 2026.
Price: development window 2021-W01 – 2024-W52; holdout 2025-W01 – 2026-W44. (Features from the
Directorate start in late 2018, so earlier origins would have almost no training data.)
Holdout results are not computed or looked at before the analysis freeze (04:00 on 8 Nov 2026).
At the freeze the full backtest runs once. F1 and F2 are judged on the holdout; results for the
development window are reported alongside.

FORECAST TESTS (THE MAIN FOCUS)
F1 (primary, production). On the holdout, the Drivers model's nowcast of each area's harvest
volume (log kg) has at least 5% lower mean absolute error than B1, and the Clark–West test
(squared error, averaged across areas each month, Newey–West with 3 lags) rejects at 5%,
one-sided. Fish count and average weight are reported separately.
F2 (secondary, price). On the holdout, at 4 weeks ahead, c1 (c0 plus the drivers below) has at least
5% lower mean absolute error than c0 (past prices, export volume, average harvest weight,
EUR/NOK), and the Clark–West test (squared error, HAC with h − 1 lags) rejects at 5%,
one-sided. c1 adds national mobile lice, treatments, disease share, temperature bands, biomass,
feed per kg of biomass, deaths per 1,000 fish and the USD/NOK change. The other horizons
(2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8) are reported with a Holm correction.

MECHANISM TESTS (HOW DRIVERS MOVE PRODUCTION)
Panel: fixed effects for area × calendar month and month × year; Driscoll–Kraay errors,
bandwidth 12; controls: biomass and fish-number anomalies four months earlier.
M1 (lice signature). Holding temperature bands and treatments fixed, the sum of mobile-lice
coefficients at lags 0–3 months is above zero for log fish harvested and below zero for log
average weight, each with a 95% interval that excludes zero.
M2 (growth). In the temperature-only model, the sum of coefficients on the degree-day anomaly
below 14 °C (bands below 8 °C and 8–14 °C combined) at lags 1–3 months is above zero for log
average weight, with a 95% interval that excludes zero.
M3 (exploratory, price). Lice pressure lowers price at 0–6 weeks and raises it at 13–26 weeks;
both one-sided tests must reject.
Driver ranking (exploratory, no pass mark): the rise in walk-forward error over Jan 2021 –
Sep 2026 when each driver group is left out, computed at the freeze.

Lag windows are fixed above, not chosen from data. Price lag lengths are chosen on 2012–2018
data only.
```
