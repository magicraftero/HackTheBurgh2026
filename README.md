## Pricing results to display after merge

**Use SISALMONI v2:** [display handoff](docs/PRICE_DISPLAY_HANDOFF.md).
The frontend entry point is `web/public/data/price_display_manifest.json`, which
points to `web/public/data/price_sisalmoni_v2.json` and its matching chart files.
These results are **SISALMONI 3–6 kg, EUR/kg, price-only Ridge v2**, evaluated
retrospectively on 24 weeks. The older `price.json` is a separate **SSB/NOK** test.

The Ridge experiment is the result to present; its experimental status and the
separate no-change live recommendation must remain visible. The handoff gives
the exact fields, labels, percentages and frontend integration notes.
